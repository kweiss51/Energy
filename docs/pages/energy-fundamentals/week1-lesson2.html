Lesson 2: Types of Energy & Global Flows

Learning Goal

Identify the main types of energy and trace their journey through the global system.

Hero’s Problem

You know energy exists, but what kinds are there? How does energy move from source to socket?

Lesson Content

There are many types of energy, but the most important for our world are:

  • Mechanical: Motion (wind, water, machines)
  • Chemical: Stored in fuels, batteries, food
  • Thermal: Heat (engines, stoves)
  • Electrical: Flows of electrons (power lines)
  • Radiant: Light (solar panels, the sun)

Globally, energy flows from sources (coal, sun, wind, water) through conversion (power plants), transmission (the grid), and finally to you, the user. Each step has losses and challenges.

Story Element

You’re on a journey from a coal mine in Wyoming, to a wind farm in Texas, to your own home. Each stop reveals a new form of energy and a new challenge.
