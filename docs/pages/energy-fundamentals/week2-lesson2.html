Lesson 2: The Laws of Thermodynamics (and Why They Matter)

Learning Goal

Grasp the basic laws of thermodynamics and their impact on energy systems.

Hero’s Problem

Why can’t we make a 100% efficient engine? Why do we lose energy as heat?

Lesson Content

The First Law of Thermodynamics says energy can’t be created or destroyed—only changed from one form to another. The Second Law says every energy conversion increases entropy (disorder), and some energy is always lost as heat.

This is why no engine, power plant, or battery is perfectly efficient. A coal plant might convert only 35% of the fuel’s energy into electricity; the rest is lost as heat. Even your body is only about 25% efficient at turning food into motion.

Understanding these laws helps us design better systems, reduce waste, and make smarter choices about energy use.

Story Element

You’re the hero, trying to build a perpetual motion machine. The laws of physics stand in your way—but they also guide you to smarter solutions.
