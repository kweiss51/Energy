What is Energy?

Energy is the ability to do work. It powers everything in our world—from the sun shining and the wind blowing, to the lights in your home and the phone in your hand. Energy comes in many forms: mechanical, chemical, thermal, electrical, and radiant. Understanding energy is the first step to mastering the systems that shape our future.
