Lesson 1: From Source to Power Plant

Learning Goal

Understand the journey from raw energy sources to usable power.

Hero’s Problem

You know energy comes from somewhere, but how does coal, sunlight, or wind become electricity?

Lesson Content

Every form of energy we use starts as a raw resource. Coal is mined, sunlight shines, wind blows, water flows. But none of these are directly usable in your home—they must be converted.

Power plants are the conversion engines of our world. A coal plant burns fuel to boil water, creating steam that spins a turbine. A wind farm uses the wind’s motion to turn blades, which spin a generator. Solar panels convert sunlight directly into electricity using the photovoltaic effect.

Each technology has its own strengths and weaknesses. Fossil fuels are reliable but polluting. Renewables are clean but variable. Nuclear is powerful but controversial. The challenge is to balance these sources to meet society’s needs.

Story Element

You’re the hero, standing at the gates of a power plant. Your challenge: to understand the magic that turns raw resources into the electricity that powers your life.
