Lesson 3: Comparing Generation Technologies

Learning Goal

Compare the pros, cons, and impacts of major generation technologies.

Hero’s Problem

With so many options—coal, gas, nuclear, solar, wind—how do we choose the best way to generate electricity?

Lesson Content

Each generation technology has trade-offs:

  • Coal: Reliable, cheap, but high emissions.
  • Natural Gas: Flexible, lower emissions than coal, but still fossil-based.
  • Nuclear: Low emissions, high output, but expensive and controversial.
  • Hydro: Clean, reliable, but site-limited and can impact ecosystems.
  • Wind & Solar: Clean, renewable, but variable and need storage or backup.

The best mix depends on local resources, policy, and demand. The future is moving toward cleaner, more flexible systems—but every choice has consequences.

Story Element

You’re the hero, tasked with powering a city. Each technology is a tool in your kit. Your challenge: build a system that’s reliable, affordable, and sustainable.
