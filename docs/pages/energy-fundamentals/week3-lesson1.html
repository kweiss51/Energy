Lesson 1: How Electricity Gets to You

Learning Goal

Understand the basics of transmission and distribution.

Hero’s Problem

You know electricity is generated somewhere, but how does it travel hundreds of miles to your home?

Lesson Content

Electricity travels from power plants through high-voltage transmission lines, then steps down to lower voltages for distribution to homes and businesses. Substations, transformers, and circuit breakers keep the system safe and reliable.

Transmission lines can span continents, connecting regions and balancing supply and demand. Distribution networks are the “last mile,” delivering power to your door.

Story Element

You’re the hero, following the journey of a single electron from a power plant to your phone charger.
