Week 3: The Grid and Beyond

This week, you’ll discover the invisible network that makes modern life possible: the electric grid. We’ll trace the journey of electricity from the power plant to your wall outlet, explore how the grid balances supply and demand in real time, and look at the challenges and innovations shaping the grid of the future. By the end, you’ll see the world around you in a new light—one that’s powered by a complex, dynamic, and increasingly smart system.

The grid is more than wires and poles—it’s a living system that must balance supply and demand every second. Grid operators use advanced forecasting, flexible power plants, and energy storage to keep the lights on. The future grid will be even smarter, with real-time sensors, distributed energy, and new ways for consumers to participate.
