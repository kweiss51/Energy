Lesson 1: What is Energy? (The Hero’s Problem)

Learning Goal

Understand the fundamental concept of energy and its role in the modern world.

Hero’s Problem

You’re surrounded by energy, but it’s invisible and confusing. How does it power your life? Why does it matter?

Lesson Content

Energy is the ability to do work. It’s what lights your home, powers your phone, and moves your car. But energy isn’t just electricity—it’s everywhere, in many forms: mechanical, chemical, thermal, electrical, and more.

Imagine waking up in a world without energy. No lights, no heat, no transportation. Our civilization is built on harnessing and transforming energy. Yet, most people never see the complex systems that make it possible.

Energy can’t be created or destroyed (thanks, First Law of Thermodynamics), only converted. The challenge? Every conversion loses some energy as heat. That’s why efficiency matters.

Story Element

You, the learner, are the hero. Your challenge: to see the invisible threads of energy that connect every part of your life. By the end of this lesson, you’ll see the world differently.
