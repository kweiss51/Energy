Lesson 2: Energy Storage—Why, What, and How

Learning Goal

Understand the need for, and types of, energy storage.

Hero’s Problem

What happens when the sun isn’t shining or the wind isn’t blowing? How do we keep the lights on?

Lesson Content

Energy storage balances supply and demand, smooths out variability, and increases grid resilience. Technologies include:

  • Batteries: Fast, flexible, but expensive for large-scale.
  • Pumped Hydro: Stores energy by moving water uphill.
  • Thermal Storage: Stores heat or cold for later use.
  • Flywheels, Compressed Air, and more.

Storage is key to integrating renewables and building a reliable, flexible grid.

Story Element

You’re the hero, keeping a city running through a storm. Storage is your secret weapon.
