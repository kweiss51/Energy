Integrating Renewables into the Grid

What happens when the sun sets or the wind stops? By the end of this lesson, you’ll understand how renewables are integrated into the grid and the role of storage and flexibility.

What You’ll Learn

  • Variability and challenges of renewables (NREL, IEA, EPRI)
  • Energy storage solutions (DOE, Sandia, EPRI)
  • Grid flexibility and demand response (FERC, CAISO, EIA)
  • Case studies of high-renewable grids (IEA, Stanford)
